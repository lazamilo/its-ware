Wondering how ItsWare works?

How does the ItsWare cabinet track assets?

Each ItsWare cabinet is equipped with six UHF RFID scanners. These scanners detect present assets as well as when assets are added and removed from the cabinet.

How does an ItsWare cabinet associate people with assets?

Cabinets are accessed with an Apple or Google Wallet pass granted to each user via the ItsWare companion app. The system associates assets taken or returned with the pass used to access the cabinet, so an admin can manage access independent of any other badging systems.

Does ItsWare track assets outside the cabinet?

The ItsWare system tracks asset interactions via tag NFC. As users interact with ItsWare tags to update tickets and notes associated with the asset, the system records user interaction history to ascertain ownership.

How do I see information associated with a tracked asset?

ItsWare tags act as NFC links to any information associated with their attached asset. Tapping your phone to a tag opens the asset's information in the ItsWare companion app.

Can I use ItsWare to assign tickets or notes to prototype hardware?

Yes. Think of ItsWare as Git for hardware prototyping. The cabinets are repositories, and the tags are branches. Assign tickets and notes to individual pieces of hardware the same way you do with software branches, and combine it with inventory management for real oversight of your hardware engineering process.

Does ItsWare interfere with our current processes?

Not at all. ItsWare integrates with and augments your current workflow. The asset ticketing system already integrates with Jira and Trello, with more integrations to come.

How big is an ItsWare cabinet?

Each cabinet is 6.7 ft tall, 3.6 ft wide and 1.8 ft deep. The internal cavity measures 5.5 ft tall, 3.2 ft wide and 1.6 ft deep, allowing for 28 cubic feet of storage with all shelves removed.

Are the cabinet shelves reconfigurable?

Yes. Each shelf is attached to internal rails with a two step slotting mechanism, allowing each shelf to be rearranged or removed entirely.
